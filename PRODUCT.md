# Product — 2036

## Purpose

2036 lets German-speaking players aged roughly 16+ explore how municipal decisions interact over a decade. It communicates consequences through a living 3D city, transparent metrics, and causal explanations rather than party labels.

## Current product slice

- One fictional city: Lindenhafen.
- Desktop-first browser play with WebGPU and WebGL 2 fallback.
- Strategy camera, building inspection, traffic, pedestrians, and day/night atmosphere.
- Monthly deterministic simulation and three illustrative municipal policies.
- German HUD and simulated city/national/world news.

## Principles

Visual quality, performance, clean architecture, simulation extensibility, and political-data transparency are prioritized in that order. No combat, criminal gameplay, live news, multiplayer, or individual-citizen simulation belongs in the initial campaign.

## Success

The vertical slice succeeds when players explore the city voluntarily, can enact policies, observe delayed numeric and visual consequences, and understand that prototype effect values are model assumptions rather than forecasts.
