# Data Sources

The vertical slice uses fictional values authored by the 2036 project to prove architecture and gameplay. They are displayed as **Modellannahme – keine reale Prognose** and must not be cited as evidence about a real policy or party.

Future evidence hierarchy:

1. Official statutes, budgets, election programs, and statistical offices for baselines/positions.
2. Peer-reviewed systematic reviews or strong quasi-experimental studies for effects.
3. Reputable research institutes for scenario ranges when primary evidence is unavailable.

Each claim records publication/access dates, geography, method, applicability, range, confidence, and a separate position/effect claim type.
