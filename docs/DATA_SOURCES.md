# Data Sources

The vertical slice uses fictional values authored by the 2036 project to prove architecture and gameplay. They are displayed as **Modellannahme – keine reale Prognose** and must not be cited as evidence about a real policy or party.

Future evidence hierarchy:

1. Official statutes, budgets, election programs, and statistical offices for baselines/positions.
2. Peer-reviewed systematic reviews or strong quasi-experimental studies for effects.
3. Reputable research institutes for scenario ranges when primary evidence is unavailable.

Each claim records publication/access dates, geography, method, applicability, range, confidence, and a separate position/effect claim type.

## Party-position baseline — accessed 2026-09-11

The fictional party profiles use current German politics as inspiration without presenting Lindenhafen values as real-world facts:

- [Final 2025 Bundestag election result — Federal Returning Officer](https://www.bundeswahlleiterin.de/bundestagswahlen/2025/ergebnisse/bund-99.html)

- [Green 2025 government program — Bündnis 90/Die Grünen](https://www.gruene.de/artikel/zusammen-wachsen)

Official programs establish party positions only. They do not establish the magnitude or direction of simulated policy effects. Effect values remain separately labeled prototype assumptions until empirical effect evidence is added.
