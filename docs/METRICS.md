# Metrics

## Implemented raw metrics

Population, employment rate, housing units, average asking rent, public-transport coverage, available city budget, emissions index, and citizen satisfaction.

## Implemented health scores

Economy, employment, housing, education, healthcare, infrastructure, safety, social cohesion, environment, cost of living, fiscal health, and satisfaction. Scores are normalized to `0–100`, where `100` is always the preferred civic outcome.

Vertical-slice normalization is intentionally provisional and labeled as such. Changing a formula requires a content-version bump and deterministic regression tests.
