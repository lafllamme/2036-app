## Why these files and not the originals

Freesound serves full-quality downloads only to signed-in users, and these are the preview renders,
which it serves openly: Ogg Vorbis at about 128 kbit/s. For a bed that sits underneath a game and is
filtered and faded before it reaches anybody, that is not a compromise worth the account — and Ogg
rather than MP3 because a Vorbis stream loops without the silence an MP3 encoder pads it with.

Nothing was re-encoded. The search was restricted to sounds of twenty to forty seconds so that the
previews are already the right length and the right size: 1.7 MB for the five together.

| `wind.ogg` | freesound.org | florianreichelt | [459981 — strong wind](https://freesound.org/people/florianreichelt/sounds/459981/) |
| `thunder.ogg` | freesound.org | Fission9 | [581125 — distant thunder](https://freesound.org/people/Fission9/sounds/581125/) |

Two rain recordings rather than one because light rain is a different sound from heavy rain, not the
same sound quieter: `drizzle` carries the beginning and the end of every shower and `rain` fades up
over it in the middle. Snow has no recording because snow makes no sound — what a player hears in it
is the wind, and the city losing its top end. See `citySounds.ts`.
